About Us

Our Story: Designing SanctuariesBlessed Decor was founded in 2023 by CHARLES PECULIAR with a simple, yet profound vision: To transform houses into truly blessed homes.
We believe that your environment profoundly impacts your well-being, and every space—from a grand living room to a quiet reading nook—should be a sanctuary.
We recognized that many clients feel overwhelmed by the process of design, struggling to translate their personal style into a cohesive, functional space. From that realization, Blessed Decor was born.
What started as a small studio focused on residential remodels, has grown into a trusted design firm dedicated to creating spaces that are both beautiful and deeply personal.

Our Mission & Philosophy At Blessed Decor, we are driven by the core belief that inspired design doesn't just look good—it feels good.
Our mission is simple: To create timeless, harmonious interior spaces for our clients by blending sophisticated aesthetics with functional, intuitive living.
Harmony: We focus on the balance of light, texture, and form to create a peaceful environment.
Personalization: Your space should tell your story. We listen intently to your desires and lifestyle needs.
Excellence: We partner with skilled artisans and use high-quality materials to ensure lasting beauty and value.

What We DoWe specialize in full-service interior design, catering to discerning homeowners, boutique commercial clients, luxury residential remodels, and as many wants something exquisite in their personal space.

Services Conceptual Design & Space Planning: Optimizing layout and flow for maximum functionality.
Material & Finish Selection: Curating palettes that bring warmth and elegance.
Furniture, Lighting, & Decor Sourcing: Selecting unique pieces that reflect a 'blessed' aesthetic.
Project Management: Overseeing the entire design process from concept to installation.
